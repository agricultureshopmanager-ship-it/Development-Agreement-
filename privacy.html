Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to ShopSteward ("we," "our," or "us"). ShopSteward is a Sole Proprietorship business operated by Samiran Das. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a positive experience on our website (shopsteward.in) and while using our custom shop management software. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect information about you in a variety of ways, including:

  • Personal Data: Name, email address, phone number, shop name, business type, and billing address.
  • Payment Data: We use Cashfree as our third-party payment gateway. We do not store your credit card, UPI, or net banking details on our servers.
  • Shop Data: Inventory details, sales records, customer names, phone numbers, and udhaar/credit records entered by you into the software.
  • Technical Data: IP address, browser type, operating system, and access times. Our website frontend is hosted on GitHub Pages.
  • AI Interaction Data: Questions or prompts you submit to the AI Agent, along with the shop data used to generate the response.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain our software and services.
  • Process your transactions and manage your subscription via Cashfree.
  • Generate reports, invoices, and AI-driven insights.
  • Respond to your comments, questions, and provide customer support.
  • Send you technical notices, updates, security alerts, and administrative messages.
  • Comply with legal obligations under Indian law.

4. AI Agent & Data Processing

Our software includes an AI Agent powered by Google Gemini. When you ask questions, the AI processes your shop data to provide answers. We do not authorize Google to use your shop's sensitive financial or customer data to train their public AI models. Your data remains your property.

5. Disclosure of Your Information

We may share your information with:

  • Service Providers: Third-party vendors who perform services on our behalf (e.g., GitHub for hosting, Cashfree for payment processing, Google for AI processing).
  • Legal Requirements: If required by law, court order, or governmental regulation.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information or your shop's data to third parties for marketing purposes. We do not use any third-party analytics or tracking tools (such as Google Analytics) on our website.

6. Data Security & Backups

We implement reasonable technical and organizational security measures to protect your data. We perform automatic backups every 24 hours. However, no electronic transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Retention

We retain your personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy. If you cancel your subscription, we will retain your data for 30 days before securely deleting it, unless a longer retention period is required by law.

8. Your Rights (DPDP Act 2023 Compliance)

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you have the right to:

  • Access, correct, or update your personal data.
  • Request the erasure of your personal data.
  • Withdraw your consent to data processing at any time.
  • Nominate another individual to exercise your rights in case of death or incapacity.

To exercise these rights, please contact us at the details provided in Section 12.

9. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our website does not use tracking cookies or analytics. We may use essential browser storage (local storage) solely for the functionality of the web application (such as maintaining your login session).

10. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party sites (e.g., WhatsApp, Cashfree). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these third-party sites. We encourage you to read their privacy policies.

11. Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for business owners and are not directed to individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
